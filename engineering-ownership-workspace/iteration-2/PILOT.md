# Pilot, not a result

One run per configuration on a single scenario. Do not read the pass rates in
`benchmark.md` as a measurement: n=1 gives a standard deviation of zero by
construction, and the expectations these runs were graded against are the
original ones, which have not yet been rewritten to be passable on merit by a
baseline.

What this pilot was for, and what it found, is recorded in the change brief for
`defensible-skill-evaluation`.
