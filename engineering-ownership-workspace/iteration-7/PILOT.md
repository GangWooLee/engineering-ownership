# Efficacy measurement, in progress

This is the first iteration that asks what the skill does rather than whether it
runs. Everything before it measured invocation, harness behaviour, or fixtures.

Do not read a figure from this directory until this note says the collection
finished and reports what was excluded. A partially collected sweep looks exactly
like a complete one from the outside, and the earlier rate-limited stretch showed
that runs can vanish in contiguous blocks rather than at random - which is the
shape most likely to bias a mean if it is read early.
